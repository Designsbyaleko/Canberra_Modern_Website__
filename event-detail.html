The Shine Dome Nocturne.

A rare after-hours tour of Sir Roy Grounds' 1959 dome, led by the conservation team responsible for its restoration.

Date
Monday, 2 November 2026
Time
18:30 to 21:00
Location
Acton, Canberra
Price

What to expect

  • 01

    After-hours access

    Enter the Shine Dome when it is closed to the public. The building is lit from within, and the city outside recedes.

  • 02

    Guided conservation talk

    Hear from the conservation architect leading the dome's restoration on what it takes to care for a building this singular.

  • 03

    Small group tour

    Capped at 24 guests — enough to feel the energy of the space, few enough that every question gets an answer.

About this evening

The Shine Dome, formally the Australian Academy of Science, is one of the most photographed pieces of Australian modernism, and one of the most fragile. This tour begins at dusk, with the dome lit from within, and moves between the council chamber, the meeting room, and the basement archives.

You'll be guided by the conservation architect leading the dome's current restoration, and joined by Academy historians who will trace its design lineage from Le Corbusier to the present.

Programme

  • 18:30: Welcome drinks under the roof shell
  • 19:00: Curatorial walk through structure, sequence, and surface
  • 20:00: Conversation on what it means to keep a dome
  • 20:45: Closing remarks and Q&A

Practical information

Meeting point
Main entrance, Clunies Ross Street, Acton. Look for the Canberra Modern sign.
Duration
Approximately 2.5 hours
Getting there

Limited on-street parking. Closest accessible bays on Liversidge Street. Bus routes 3 and 7 stop at the Australian National University. A 10-minute walk from Acton Park ferry stop.

Access & mobility

Step-free entry via the western ramp. A single internal step (50 mm) leads to the chamber; portable ramps are available on request when booking. Accessible WC on site.

What to bring

Flat shoes and a light jacket — the chamber is cool in the evening. Cameras are welcome; tripods are not.

Booking notes

Full refunds up to 7 days before the event; transfers permitted up to 48 hours before. After that we are unable to refund individual tickets, but the booking can be gifted. Confirmation will be sent by email within 24 hours of booking.
